## 2. What NACOS Week 2027 is

NACOS Week is the flagship annual week of the association. It is **not new**: the Computer Science department has hosted it for over a decade. 2027 is the first edition run at faculty level under the NACOS FCIT-AKSU constitution.

### Theme: "Confluence"

- Confluence = **different industry sectors converging with computing.** Separate streams meeting and flowing forward with more force.
- It does **not** mean the five FCIT departments merging. That earlier framing was dropped. Do not use it.
- The previous theme "INTERSECT" is retired. Do not use it anywhere.

### Five tracks

1. Energy
2. Finance
3. Oil & Gas
4. Medicine
5. Technology (added at the organisers' request: software, AI, cybersecurity)

### Five pillars

The Trade Fair has been dropped (9 Oct 2026). Do not mention it anywhere.

| # | Pillar | Notes |
|---|--------|-------|
| 1 | Hackathon (Innovation Bootcamp) | Multi-week, about 3 weeks, built around the five tracks. Final projects are judged at the Tech Conference; the judges decide the winners. Flagship sponsorship product. |
| 2 | Football Tournament | Open-crowd pillar. Needs jerseys / full kit sponsorship. |
| 3 | Fashion & Pageantry | Crowns the Royal Council of NACOS AKSU. Open-crowd pillar. |
| 5 | Tech Conference | Talks, panels, keynotes, networking. Ties the sector tracks to real professionals. |
| 6 | Dinner & Award Night | Not the closing night; venue not yet decided. Do not describe it as off-campus or as the closing night. A night to dine and have fun, with awards for students who have shown good character, conduct and contribution. Hackathon winners are decided at the Tech Conference, not here. |

### Audience target

- 500 to 1,000 students in person per event.
- Football and Fashion & Pageantry can draw from the wider 30,000 and may exceed 1,000.
- Cumulative footfall across the week is the honest sponsor number (projected, to be measured with QR check-in).

## 4. Sponsorship

### Status

- At the stakeholders meeting the association **agreed**: Gold ₦1,500,000, Silver ₦800,000, Bronze ₦300,000.
- The President has since put forward a **revised five-tier structure**. It is a proposal **pending stakeholder and Executive Council approval**. Until approved, treat all figures as provisional and make prices easy to change (config, not hard-coded).

### Revised proposal (pending approval)

| Tier | Price | Slots | What they own |
|------|-------|-------|---------------|
| Title Partner | ₦2,000,000 | 1 | The whole week: "NACOS Week 2027: Confluence, presented by [Brand]" plus one sector track |
| Gold | ₦1,000,000 | 2 | A flagship pillar: Hackathon or Dinner & Award Night |
| Silver | ₦500,000 | 3 | A crowd pillar: Football or Fashion & Pageantry |
| Bronze | ₦200,000 | 8 | Sign-up rights, direct student access (Trade Fair booth removed with the Trade Fair) |
| Friends of Confluence | ₦100,000 | 20 | Entry level for campus and local businesses |

Full sell-out ceiling: ₦9,100,000 (Friends of Confluence raised to ₦100,000 on 9 Oct 2026). Planning assumption: about 50% sell-through.

In-kind partners (printing, catering, power, venue, jerseys, media) are credited at the matching tier by market value.

### Principles

- Sell **outcomes**, not logos: customers (sign-ups, referral codes), talent (CV bank, intern access), content (recap video, photo rights), credibility (impact report).
- Each tier must have something the tier below cannot buy.
- Only promise benefits the team can actually deliver.
- **Category exclusivity** at the top tiers (one bank, one telco, one fintech, one energy company).
- Gold and Title sponsors can set real **hackathon problem statements** for their sector track.

### Safeguards (must be respected in any feature that touches participants)

- **Student data:** CVs and contact details are shared with sponsors only with the student's explicit consent. Any registration form must include a clear opt-in.
- **Student IP:** students keep ownership of what they build at the hackathon. Sponsors get first right of conversation, not ownership.
- **Pageant ambassadors:** Royal Council members wearing sponsor sashes / acting as brand ambassadors need a written agreement.

### Target sectors

Oil & Gas and Energy (regional CSR budgets), banks and fintechs (Flutterwave, Paystack, Access Bank, UBA Foundation, Sterling, GTCO, Zenith, Stanbic are on the target list), telcos, edtech, and local campus businesses around Ikot Akpaden.

## 8. Writing and naming rules (strict)

- Always "FCIT". Never "COINFO".
- Location is **Ikot Akpaden, Mkpat Enin**. Never say AKSU or FCIT is in Uyo.
- Never call Confluence the "first NACOS Week". It is the first **faculty-level** edition.
- Never claim "first tech hub in the region" or similar unverified superlatives.
- Use the ₦ symbol for naira (U+20A6). Liberation Sans supports it if generating PDFs.
- **No em dashes** in generated copy or documents.
- Copy should be direct and concise. No filler, no AI-sounding phrasing.
- Official documents: Times New Roman, plain formatting, no decoration unless asked.
- Keep the institutional identity (CCSA, NACOS AKSU) separate from the President's personal brands. Do not mix them on NACOS pages.
- Krait is a NACOS partner (confirmed 9 Oct 2026) and is listed among past sponsors. It is not covered by the personal-brand rule above.

## 9. Guidance for the coding agent

- Do not invent dates, prices, sponsors, speakers, judges, venues or hex codes. Use clearly marked placeholders and put changeable values (prices, slots, dates, colours) in a single config file.
- Any form collecting student data needs a consent checkbox for sharing with sponsors.
- Sponsor-facing numbers must be labelled "projected" until measured.
- When unsure about a fact, ask the President rather than guessing.
