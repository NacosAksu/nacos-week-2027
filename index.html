Computing does not work alone anymore.

The grid, the bank, the oil field and the hospital all run on software now. NACOS Week 2027 puts the people who build that software in the same rooms as the people who run those industries.

NACOS Week is the flagship week of NACOS AKSU, the Nigeria Association of Computing Students at Akwa Ibom State University. The Computer Science department has hosted it for over a decade. In 2027 the Faculty of Computing and Information Technology hosts it, and the week is open to every student at AKSU, whatever their faculty.

Four industries. One university.

Every pillar of the week, from the hackathon briefs to the Conference Day panels, is organised around four sectors that shape the economy of the Niger Delta and the country.

  1. 01

    Energy

    Power generation, distribution and metering. Where data meets the grid.

  2. 02

    Finance

    Banking, payments and fintech. Software that moves money and earns trust.

  3. 03

    Oil & Gas

    Exploration, operations and safety. The industry this region is built on.

  4. 04

    Medicine

    Records, diagnostics and access to care. Computing that keeps people well.

Questions

When exactly is NACOS Week 2027?

February 2027. The exact dates are being finalised and will be published here first.

Who can attend?

Every student at Akwa Ibom State University, from any faculty. The Faculty of Computing and Information Technology hosts the week, but it is not limited to computing students. The Football Tournament and Fashion & Pageantry are open-crowd events for the whole AKSU community.

Does it cost anything?

Attendance details, including any fees, will be published when registration opens fully. Register and we will tell you first.

Who can join the hackathon?

Eligibility and team rules are published with hackathon registration, which opens before the bootcamp starts.

Who owns what we build at the hackathon?

You do. Partners get the first right to a conversation with your team, not ownership of your work.

Will my details be given to sponsors?

Only if you tick the optional box on the registration form. If you leave it unticked, your details stay with NACOS AKSU.

Is this the first NACOS Week?

No. The Computer Science department has hosted NACOS Week for over a decade. 2027 is the first edition hosted by the Faculty of Computing and Information Technology.

How does my company become a partner?

See the tiers above, download the brochure, or write to the team. Payments go only to the NACOS AKSU student body account.
