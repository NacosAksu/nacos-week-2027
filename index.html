01 — The gathering

About NACOS Week 2027

NACOS Week 2027 is a flagship technology event built around the idea of Confluence — the coming together of distinct forces to create something greater than either could achieve alone. It brings together people, ideas, skills, experiences, and different areas of technology into one shared ecosystem.

Organized by

National Association of Computing Students, Akwa Ibom State University Chapter (NACOS AKSU).

09 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can attend NACOS Week ’27? +

NACOS Week 2027 is open to students of Akwa Ibom State University, computing enthusiasts, software builders, designers, and tech practitioners from across institutions and regional hubs.

Is registration free? +

Yes. Registration is free. Seats in the main conference hall and breakout workshops are finite, so early registration reserves your place.

Where is the event holding? +

The event is holding at the Conference Hall, Akwa Ibom State University (AKSU) Main Campus, located at Ikot Akpaden, Mkpat Enin LGA, Akwa Ibom State.

What time does the event start? +

Sessions begin at 10:00 AM prompt each day from 1st to 4th February, 2027. We recommend arriving 30 minutes early for badge verification.

Can professionals attend? +

Yes. Industry practitioners, alumni, engineers, and founders are invited to attend, share insights, mentor students, and engage during the sessions.

How can I become a sponsor? +

Organizations can use the "Become a Sponsor" link or write to info@nacosaksu.ng. Partnership tiers cover session mentions, lab sponsorship, and talent access.

Will there be certificates? +

Yes. Registered attendees who participate in workshops and technical sessions will receive verified digital certificates of participation.

Who can I contact for support? +

Contact the NACOS AKSU Organizing Committee via email at info@nacosaksu.ng or connect with executive officers on campus.
